# Refund linkability, measured

Generated by `bb-auction simulate-refunds` (seed 42, 48 simulated hours, 4 losing bidders per lot). The observer
model and its limits are in `services/auction/src/linkability.rs`; in short, the observer knows every lot's close
time and sees every operator transaction's time and action count, and a refund is "linked" when its transaction
falls inside exactly one lot's refund window.

## Demo settings: delay 30 to 300 s, 60 s window, batches of up to 8

| lots/hour | refunds | naive: txs | naive: linked | batched: txs | batched: linked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 64 | 64 | 100% | 48 | 100% |
| 2 | 380 | 380 | 89% | 273 | 87% |
| 6 | 1108 | 1108 | 67% | 723 | 62% |
| 20 | 3924 | 3924 | 22% | 1820 | 16% |
| 60 | 11896 | 11896 | 1% | 2930 | 0% |

## What the numbers say

- Timing linkability is decided by the delay range and how many lots close near each other, not by batching. A
  lot that closes alone in a four-hour quiet period is linkable whatever we do; the 25% at half a lot per hour is
  exactly those lonely lots.
- Batching cuts the number of transactions an observer gets to look at (to a quarter at busy rates) and removes
  the per-bidder count signal: with one transaction per bidder, counting transactions after a close gives the
  number of losers, which can be matched to the public receipt count. A window transaction mixes lots.
- The demo settings exist so a three-minute recording can show a refund arriving. They are not private at demo
  lot densities and the recording should say so. Production defaults in `deploy/auction.env.example` are the
  10 min to 4 h range with a 10 min window.
- Not modelled, and still a leak at low density: the action count of a batched transaction (visible on chain)
  bounds how many refunds it carries; and refund amounts equal the lot's fixed deposit, so a lot with an unusual
  deposit would be identifiable by amount if an output is ever unshielded. Deposits are therefore drawn from one
  short menu (0.001 to 5 ZEC in a 1-2-5 series, `services/auction/src/denom.rs`; `ALLOW_ANY_DEPOSIT=true`
  overrides), so a 0.05 ZEC refund looks like every other 0.05 ZEC refund. Splitting refunds into denominations
  was rejected: each extra output costs a ZIP-317 action fee and the piece count leaks what the amount would.

Reproduce: `bb-auction simulate-refunds --delay-min 30 --delay-max 300 --window-secs 60` and
`bb-auction simulate-refunds`.
