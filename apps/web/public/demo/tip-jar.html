Why the dry season came early

The rains stopped three weeks before anyone expected. By the second week of October the river had dropped to the stones, and the farmers who had planted late were already counting what they would lose.

I walked the lower fields on Tuesday. The maize that went in on time is fine. Everything planted after the first rain is a gamble now, and most of it will be lost. The lesson is old and nobody likes it: plant on the calendar, not on hope.
